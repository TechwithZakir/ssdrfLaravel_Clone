"Self Mastery" (for Professionals)-9th Batch

  • Home
  • Course Details

Self-mastery is a journey, not a destination. It requires ongoing effort, self-compassion, and patience. Different people may focus on different aspects of self-mastery based on their individual goals and circumstances. Whether you're working on personal development, professional success, or overall life satisfaction, self-mastery can contribute significantly to your journey

Self-mastery is a signature online course design and organized by Institute of Soft Skills Development and Research, Bangladesh (issdr,b) an initiative of “SSDR Foundation” a government registered non-profit organization

This course is designed to assist you on your path. It will point out the pitfalls and offer suggestions to support your efforts in personal development.

8th Batch will be started on 8th October-2025

COURSE OUTLINE

MODULE-1: SELF AWARENESS

Lession-1 : Values, belief and attitude

Lession-2 : Know yourself

Lession-3 : Living with your purpose

MODULE-2: SELF SISCIPLINE

Lession-4 : Building Self-Discipline

Lession-5 : Emotional Intelligence

Lession-6 : Habit Reprogramming

MODULE-3: SELF DEVELOPMENT

Lession-7 : Improving Communication Skill

Lession-8 : Cognitive Reframing

Lession-9 : Purpose Driven Leadership

MODULE-3: SELF REALIZATION

Lession-10 : Success, happiness & peace in life 

 

HOW PARTICIPANTS WILL BE BENIFITED

1. Participants can be self aware and build their self confident

2. They can find their purpose and set their SMART goal

3. Participants can learn how to reprogram their habit

4. They can manage their mind, emotion and stress as well and can be more productive in their workplace

5. Participants will get effective communication method by applying these method they will be better communicator than before.

6. Participants can increase their ability to solve the problem with creative and critical thinking.

7. They can learn about compassionate leadership skills and can lead their team to achieve organizational goal in effective and efficient way.
