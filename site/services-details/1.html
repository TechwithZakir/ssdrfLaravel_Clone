Team Power: Building High Trust, High Performance Teams

  • Home
  • Course Details

Learning Outcomes

·         Understand how teams work and how to improve collaboration

·         Develop effective communication & feedback skills

·         Learn techniques for solving problems together

·         Handle conflicts positively

·         Build trust and emotional intelligence

·         Create an actionable plan to improve team performance

SESSION BREAKUP (8 Hours)

MODULE 1: Foundation of Teamwork

 

1.1 What is a Team?

  • Team vs. Group
  • Characteristics of an effective team
  • Why teams fail in organizations

 

1.2 Stages of Team Development (Tuckman Model)

  • Forming
  • Storming
  • Norming
  • Performing
  • Real-life examples for each stage

 

1.3 Team Roles & Strengths

  • Belbin’s Team Roles
  • Mapping individual strengths
  • Aligning roles with tasks

 

 Activity: The Marshmallow Challenge or Paper Tower Challenge

MODULE 2: Communication for Collaboration

 

2.1 Foundations of Effective Communication

  • Verbal & non-verbal communication
  • Listening vs. hearing
  • Clear message delivery model (Sender - Message - Receiver - Feedback)

 

2.2 Overcoming Communication Barriers

  • Cultural barriers
  • Emotional barriers
  • Personality differences

 

2.3 Feedback Skills

  • SBI Model (Situation-Behavior-Impact)
  • Giving constructive vs. destructive feedback
  • Receiving feedback gracefully

 

 

 

Activity: Back to Back Exercise

(Teaches clarity, instruction, listening)

MODULE 3: Trust, Respect & Emotional Intelligence

 

3.1 Building Trust in Teams

  • The Trust Triangle: Authenticity, Logic, Empathy
  • Why trust breaks & how to repair it

 

3.2 Emotional Intelligence in Teamwork

  • Self-awareness
  • Empathy
  • Managing emotions under pressure

 

3.3 Collaborative Mindset

  • Growth vs. fixed mindset
  • Celebrating diversity in teams

 

Activity: Two Truths and a Lie

(Creates bonding & trust)

 

MODULE 4: Problem Solving & Conflict Management

 

4.1 Understanding Conflict

  • Healthy vs. unhealthy conflict
  • Conflict styles (Thomas Kilmann Model)
  • Avoiding office politics & blame games

 

4.2 Team Problem-Solving Models

  • 5 Whys
  • Root cause analysis
  • Brainstorming guidelines

 

4.3 Collaborative Decision-Making

  • Consensus vs. majority
  • Decision-making matrix

 

Activity: Lost on the Moon Ranking Exercise

(Shows group vs. individual decision quality)

 

MODULE 5: Team Motivation & Ownership

 

5.1 What Drives Team Motivation?

  • Intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivators
  • The Role of Purpose, Autonomy, Mastery

 

5.2 Creating Team Accountability

  • SMART goals
  • Individual vs. team goals alignment
  • Taking ownership instead of blaming

 

5.3 Productivity & Team Efficiency

  • Delegation frameworks
  • Time management tools
  • Collaboration tools (Trello, Asana, Slack examples)

 

Activity: The Delegation Game

 

MODULE 6: Action Planning & Commitment

 

6.1 Team Action Plan Framework

  • Identify gaps
  • Define commitments
  • Set short-term & long-term goals

 

6.2 Building a High-Performance Team Culture

  • Trust, transparency, accountability
  • Recognition & appreciation culture

 

6.3 Final Team Charter

  • Ground rules
  • Expectations
  • Next 30-day plan

 

 

Activity: Create Your Team Charter
